## Final claim — and what it means

I am NOT 100% sure about U1-U6. I AM confident in the 20 concrete changes, their file:line locations, and that each has a documented Polymarket spec or a reference-impl verbatim source backing it.

The plan is ready in the sense that:

- Every code change site is identified.
- Every Polymarket counterpart is documented or live-probed.
- The unknowns are bounded and have explicit mitigations.

The plan is NOT ready in the sense that:

- The V2 Order EIP-712 signed struct is encapsulated by the SDK; we don't hand-roll it. If the SDK has a bug that matters at our scale, we'd discover it post-launch.
- Some contract addresses (Safe factory, UMA adapters) need verification at impl time, not now.
- Sports WS shape needs a live probe at impl time.

These are the risks I'm willing to take into Stage 1. The user should know about them before saying "go."
